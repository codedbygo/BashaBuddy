The problem

Getting a basic certificate often means many trips, an agent's fee, or giving up.

  • Information is scattered across central portals, state portals and office notice boards.
  • It is written in formal English or bureaucratic language, on sites built for desktops.
  • People find out what is missing at the counter. One forgotten document means another trip, another day of lost wages.
  • Agents fill the gap and charge for information that should be free.
  • Text-first apps exclude people who are more comfortable speaking than typing, in their own language.
  • Chatbots that guess are dangerous here: a confident wrong answer about eligibility costs real money and time.

Our users

People who need public services most, and find the websites hardest.

Students

Scholarships, income, caste and residence certificates for admissions. Usually the family member who handles the paperwork.

Farmers

Income support and land-linked schemes. Often on a basic Android phone, more at ease speaking Telugu or Hindi than typing.

Senior citizens and women

Old age and widow pensions, maternity support. Often depend on relatives or agents to find out what to bring.

Example user: a mother in a Telangana village whose daughter needs an income certificate for a scholarship. She wants to know, in Telugu, where to go and what to carry, before she spends a day travelling.

The product

Speak naturally. Check what was heard. Get a simple answer. Listen to it.

1

Choose a language: తెలుగు, हिंदी or English. The whole interface switches.

2

Tap the mic and ask, e.g. "What documents do I need for a ration card?"

3

Confirm the transcript. Edit it or record again. Nothing is sent unchecked.

4

Get the answer: documents, eligibility or steps, with the official source. Tap to hear it.

  • Follow-ups work: after asking about scholarships, "what documents?" answers for scholarships.
  • Works without a mic: typing and one-tap sample questions run the same flow.
  • Mobile-first: large touch targets, Telugu and Devanagari fonts, works on small Android screens.

Trust by design

In public services, an honest "I don't know" beats a confident guess.

  • Answers only from a structured dataset. No language model inventing eligibility rules, amounts or deadlines.
  • Every answer is labelled "Demo, not verified" and points to the official portal.
  • Unknown service? It says it has no verified information, instead of answering something generic.
  • Never asks for OTPs, passwords, PINs or bank details, and warns users who offer them.
  • Never claims to submit or track applications. It is not connected to government databases.
  • No accounts, no stored recordings, no personal data. Emergencies are pointed to 112.

What works today

A working prototype, live and tested.

  • 9 services: scholarships, income, caste and residence certificates, ration card, PM-KISAN, pensions, maternity support, Aadhaar
  • Full interface and answers in Telugu, Hindi and English
  • Voice input with transcript confirmation, read-aloud answers
  • Safety handling: sensitive data, emergencies, prompt-injection attempts
  • Live on Vercel; starts locally with one command: docker compose up
  • Automated tests for intents, data honesty, safety, validation and transcription errors
  • Handles failures clearly: mic denied, no voice on device, server unreachable
  • Honest limit Scheme data is demo data and must be verified before real use

Launch plan

Start where trust is built: one state, real data, real counters.

Months 0 to 2

Verified data

  • Verify the top 20 services for Telangana and Andhra Pradesh against official portals
  • Record source and date for every fact
  • Move to Whisper "small" for better Telugu
Months 2 to 4

Pilot

  • One college scholarship cell and one citizen service centre or NGO
  • Volunteers help first-time users
  • Measure: questions answered, wrong-answer reports, trips saved
Months 4 to 9

Scale

  • Add more Indian languages
  • WhatsApp and phone (IVR) access for basic phones
  • Partner with state departments to keep data current

Success means fewer wasted trips: users arrive at the counter with the right documents the first time.
